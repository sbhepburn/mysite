The Secret Price-Tag Code

Costco almost never shouts about a sale. No big banners, no colored signs — instead, the last two digits of the price do the talking, and a tiny corner symbol tells you when to pounce. Learn to read the bottom-right of every tag and you'll shop like an employee.

The AsteriskA.K.A. "The Death Star"

A small ✱ in the top-right corner means Costco isn't reordering it — the item is being discontinued (or it's seasonal and headed out). Once the warehouse sells through, it's very likely gone for good. See it on something you love? Buy your backups today. Often you'll spot it alongside a .97, meaning it's discounted on its way out the door.

📅 The Date Stamp

Bottom-right of the tag = when the price last changed. If a .97 has been sitting for a couple of weeks and shelves are still full, another markdown may be on the way. Feeling lucky? Wait it out — but it might sell first.

🟢 The Green Tag

White is the default tag color. The one exception you'll see is a green tag, which flags an organic item — handy, though a few warehouses haven't fully adopted the practice yet.

Food Court Legends

Prices frozen in time. While fast food has jumped 30%+ since 2020, the court has barely flinched.

A quarter-pound all-beef Kirkland dog + a 20-oz drink with free refills — unchanged for 40 years and called "sacrosanct" by execs. New for 2026: swap the soda for a Kirkland water at the same price. Costco's single best-seller, with ~245 million sold last fiscal year.

Evergreen Member Wins

The deals that don't need a coupon book — they're baked into how Costco operates.

KS

Kirkland Signature

The house brand built to match or beat national names — and frequently made in the very same factories. Reliable value across coffee, batteries, olive oil, paper goods, and more.

↩

The Legendary Return Policy

The "risk-free 100% satisfaction guarantee." Most items have no time limit; electronics get 90 days. It even covers clearance and discontinued buys — so trying that ✱ item is low-risk.

⛽

The Gas Pump

Member fuel typically runs ~10–15¢ a gallon under nearby stations. For regular fill-ups, the savings alone can chip away at your membership fee over a year.

2%

Executive 2% Reward

The upgraded membership earns 2% back as a year-end certificate. Spend enough (roughly $230+/month) and that reward basically refunds the cost of the membership itself.

30

The 30-Day Price Adjustment

Bought something that went on sale within 30 days? Take your receipt to the returns desk and they'll refund the difference — no need to re-buy it.

★ Three Insider Habits

  • ✱Shop in person. Costco.com prices usually run higher than the warehouse, and there's no price-matching between online and in-store.
  • ✱Scan the corners, not the products. Train your eye to register the price ending and the asterisk as you walk — that's how the deals find you.
  • ✱Move fast on clearance. The .97 you walk past on Thursday morning may genuinely be gone by Thursday afternoon.
